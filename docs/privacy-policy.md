# PhotoStyler Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 6 September 2026
**Applies to:** PhotoStyler for iPhone
**Provider:** ML Creative Studios

## The short version

PhotoStyler does not collect, transmit, or store any of your personal data.
Everything the app does happens on your iPhone. There is no account, no
analytics, no advertising, and no third-party services. Your photos never leave
your device by way of this app.

## What the app accesses, and why

**Camera.** PhotoStyler uses the camera to show a live viewfinder and to take
photos. Camera frames are processed on your device to preview a style and are
never recorded, uploaded, or sent anywhere.

**Adding to your photo library.** When you take a photo, PhotoStyler saves it to
your photo library. The app requests add-only access, which lets it save new
photos but does not let it read, browse, or modify photos already in your
library.

You can change either permission at any time in
**Settings → Privacy & Security**, or in **Settings → PhotoStyler**. Denying
camera access stops the app taking photos; it does not affect anything else.

## What the app stores

PhotoStyler keeps its own copy of photos you take, inside the app's private
container on your iPhone, along with a note of which style you applied. This is
what the app's "My Photos" screen shows.

This data is private to the app. It is not readable by other apps, and it is not
sent anywhere. Deleting a photo in "My Photos" removes the app's copy; the copy
in your photo library is unaffected and must be deleted from the Photos app.
Deleting PhotoStyler removes all of the app's stored data.

## What the app does not do

- It does not collect personal information
- It does not create accounts or ask you to sign in
- It does not use analytics, crash reporting, or advertising services
- It does not include third-party SDKs
- It does not track you across apps or websites
- It makes no network requests

Because no data is collected, none is shared, sold, or disclosed to anyone.

## Children

PhotoStyler is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone,
including children under 13.

## Backups

If you use iCloud Backup or an encrypted local backup, your iPhone's backup may
include the app's stored photos, in the same way it includes other app data.
That backup is governed by Apple's terms and your device settings, not by this
app.
